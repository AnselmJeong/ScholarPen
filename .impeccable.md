## Design Context

### Users
Academic authors working with long Korean and English manuscripts in ScholarPen on macOS. The current request is to navigate the active manuscript through a compact heading outline while continuing to write.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, readable, precise. Extend the existing writing workspace without distracting from manuscript content.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the user's supplied outline screenshot as the interaction and density reference: a narrow right sidebar, nested titles, disclosure chevrons, and a restrained active-row highlight. Retain ScholarPen's existing fonts, lavender theme, and light/dark colors rather than introduce a new visual identity.

### Design Principles
- Keep the manuscript primary and navigation compact.
- Express hierarchy with indentation, spacing, and disclosure controls.
- Let long Korean and English titles wrap naturally.
- Keep navigation reversible and independent of manuscript content.
- Preserve usable writing space in split panes and narrow windows.
